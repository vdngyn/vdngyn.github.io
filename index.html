Hi, I'm

Vu Nguyen



I'm a graduate student in Mathematics & Statistics at Syracuse University. In 2025, I completed bachelor's degrees in Mathematics and Performance Studies at New York University's Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences and Tisch School of the Arts.

Broadly, I'm interested in how randomness interacts with large & noisy systems to produce rare events.

Specifically, I use the tools of probability & statistical learning theory to study the asymptotic behavior of stochastic processes. In practice, these problems often involve massive quantities of information, so I've become invested in the synthesis of "good" data that can be used to model and predict the tail-ends of probability measures.
